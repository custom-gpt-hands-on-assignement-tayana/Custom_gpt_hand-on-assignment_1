# Smart Resume & Interview Coach GPT

## Problem Statement

Finding a job is challenging for many students and professionals. They often struggle with creating ATS-friendly resumes, preparing for technical interviews, writing cover letters, and choosing the right learning path.

Most AI assistants provide generic responses without understanding the user's skills, experience, or career goals.

A Custom GPT can solve this problem by acting as a personalized AI career coach that provides role-specific guidance.

---

# GPT Name

Smart Resume & Interview Coach GPT

---

# Target Users

- College Students
- Fresh Graduates
- Software Developers
- IT Professionals
- Career Switchers

---

# Proposed Solution

The Smart Resume & Interview Coach GPT is designed to help users throughout their job preparation journey.

It can:

- Review resumes
- Improve ATS scores
- Generate personalized cover letters
- Conduct mock interviews
- Recommend technical projects
- Suggest learning roadmaps
- Optimize LinkedIn profiles
- Provide career guidance

---

# Expected Outcomes

After using the GPT, users will be able to:

- Create ATS-friendly resumes
- Prepare confidently for interviews
- Improve communication skills
- Build stronger portfolios
- Save time searching for resources
- Increase their chances of getting hired

---

# Why Use a Custom GPT?

Unlike a general AI assistant, this Custom GPT is trained with career-specific instructions, interview preparation strategies, resume-writing guidelines, and industry best practices.

This makes it more accurate, focused, and personalized.

---

# Feasibility

The solution is practical because it can be implemented using:

- OpenAI Custom GPT
- Prompt Engineering
- Resume Templates
- Interview Question Database
- Career Guidance Knowledge Base

No additional coding is required for the initial version.

---

# Conclusion

The Smart Resume & Interview Coach GPT provides an intelligent, personalized platform for career preparation. It simplifies resume building, interview practice, and career planning, making job preparation faster and more effective.
