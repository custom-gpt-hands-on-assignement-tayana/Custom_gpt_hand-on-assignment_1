## Objective

The objective of this assignment is to identify a real-world problem that can be solved using a Custom GPT and define its purpose, target audience, and expected outcomes.

---

## GPT Name

Smart Resume & Interview Coach GPT

---

## Problem Statement

Many students and job seekers struggle with creating ATS-friendly resumes, preparing for technical interviews, writing cover letters, and planning their careers. Existing AI tools often provide generic responses that are not tailored to individual users.

This Custom GPT solves these problems by providing personalized career guidance, resume reviews, interview preparation, and learning roadmaps.

---

## Target Users

- College Students
- Fresh Graduates
- Software Engineers
- Career Switchers
- Job Seekers

---

## Features

- ATS Resume Review
- Resume Improvement Suggestions
- Cover Letter Generation
- Technical Interview Preparation
- HR Interview Questions
- LinkedIn Profile Optimization
- Personalized Career Roadmap
- Project Recommendations

---

## Expected Outcomes

- Better resumes
- Higher interview confidence
- Personalized career guidance
- Faster job preparation
- Improved hiring chances
